# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chrona is for people who want a personal steward that looks after ongoing matters: it keeps context, runs the apps the user approves, reminds and reports, and keeps the user in control. Today, conversations (web and QQ) and apps are the product centre.

## Product Purpose

Chrona is a local-first personal steward: a kernel that runs approved apps (modules) and a Provider that talks with the user, builds and changes those apps, and works through their tools. Goals: [steward goals](docs/zh/steward-goals.md); architecture: [current architecture](docs/zh/architecture.md).

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. Chrona should feel like a reliable operating surface for work in motion: clear state, low drama, explicit recovery paths, and strong confidence that automation stays under user control.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS AI gloss: vague purple gradients, hero metrics, decorative dashboards, inflated claims, and motion that does not explain state. Avoid chat-only UI patterns that hide durable state, permission boundaries or commitments. Avoid dense technical surfaces that hide what happened, why it matters, evidence, status or the next action.

## Design Principles

1. Make current state unmistakable: decisions, meaningful changes, commitments, suggestions, results and primary actions precede execution detail.
2. Keep automation accountable: every AI action should expose source context, status, ownership, failure mode, and recovery path.
3. Favor inspectable density over decoration: dense panels are acceptable when they help users decide what to do next.
4. Preserve user authority: suggestions are not grants; generated tools are not enabled tools; sent notifications are not acknowledgments. Schedule distinguishes confirmed commitments, steward checks and tentative suggestions.
5. Reduce uncertainty with precise copy: labels should describe what will happen, what is waiting, and what the user can do now.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as baseline. Maintain readable contrast for dense text and muted labels, complete keyboard access for navigation and task actions, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation, and state indicators that do not rely on color alone.
