# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chrona is for people who want ongoing affairs looked after: retain context, observe authorized changes, surface decisions and commitments, and keep the user in control. Today / Affairs / Schedule / Delegations / Connections are the product centre; managed execution is a retained secondary capability.

## Product Purpose

Chrona is a proactive personal steward with a controllable web affairs centre and durable event loop. The canonical [personal-steward direction](docs/zh/personal-steward.md), effective 2026-10-13, supersedes earlier execution-first and result-workspace product goals. [Current implementation and gaps](docs/en/personal-steward.md) separate the local rebuild from the target: no steward deployment, real-data migration, live Reddit connection or external notification channel is claimed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. Chrona should feel like a reliable operating surface for work in motion: clear state, low drama, explicit recovery paths, and strong confidence that automation stays under user control.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS AI gloss: vague purple gradients, hero metrics, decorative dashboards, inflated claims, and motion that does not explain state. Avoid chat-only UI patterns that hide durable affairs, permission boundaries or commitments. Avoid dense technical surfaces that hide what happened, why it matters, evidence, status or the next action.

## Design Principles

1. Make current state unmistakable: decisions, meaningful changes, commitments, suggestions, results and primary actions precede execution detail.
2. Keep automation accountable: every AI action should expose source context, status, ownership, failure mode, and recovery path.
3. Favor inspectable density over decoration: dense panels are acceptable when they help users decide what to do next.
4. Preserve user authority: suggestions are not grants; generated tools are not enabled tools; sent notifications are not acknowledgments. Schedule distinguishes confirmed commitments, steward checks and tentative suggestions.
5. Reduce uncertainty with precise copy: labels should describe what will happen, what is waiting, and what the user can do now.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as baseline. Maintain readable contrast for dense text and muted labels, complete keyboard access for navigation and task actions, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation, and state indicators that do not rely on color alone.
